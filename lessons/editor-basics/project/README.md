# Meet the Editor

You don't need to know anything to start here. This lesson is a tour of the **editor**, the
program you'll use to write code, and you'll practice the things every programmer does all day:
making files and folders, typing, saving, renaming, deleting, finding things, and running a
program.

**Want a guide?** Press the **Dozer** button at the top. Dozer is our team's plow robot, and he'll
walk you through each part below, one step at a time, right on your screen. Pick any guide and
do what he says. You can't break anything.

## Your five jobs

Each one is a **checkpoint**. Open the lesson chip at the top and press **Verify** when you've
done it, and it will tell you what it sees.

1. **Make a folder** called `my-team`. (Use small letters and a dash, no spaces.)
2. **Make a file** inside it called `about.txt`, and type a sentence about our team.
3. **Rename** `untitled.txt` to `team.txt`.
4. **Delete** `delete-me.txt`.
5. **Run a program.** Open `src/Main.java`, replace the three dots with your name, and click
   **Run** (the triangle at the top right of the file).

## Stuck?

- Typed the wrong name? Rename it: click it once and press **F2**.
- Made something in the wrong place? Select it and press **Delete**, then try again.
- Want to start over? Switch away from this lesson and back again to reset it.
